# POUR / PLAY — The Cinematic Coffee Ritual Experience

A high-fidelity, scroll-driven cinematic web application recreating the concept from `@ENKYWEBSTUDIO` featuring the three core coffee rituals: **GRIND**, **POUR**, and **SIP**.

## Overview

- **Section 1: GRIND**
  - Oversized display headline with precision 3D burr grinder.
  - Cascading 3D coffee beans tumbling into the stainless steel hopper.
  - Scroll-scrubbed mechanical exploded view: casing halves separate outwards to expose the internal 48mm conical burr cutting flutes and a shower of fine coffee grounds raining down.
- **Section 2: POUR**
  - Seamless cinematic camera glide from grinder to pour-over station.
  - Stainless steel gooseneck kettle tilts into pouring position with an arched water stream into a clear ribbed V60 glass cone.
  - Coffee bloom bed swells as fresh amber coffee streams into the glass carafe below, complete with soft rising steam wisps.
- **Section 3: SIP (FIND YOUR CUP)**
  - Transition to artisanal wheel-thrown ceramic stoneware mug with two-tone reactive glaze and liquid coffee surface ripples.
  - Three interactive glassmorphic cards: `ORIGIN`, `ROAST` (with temperature & intensity meter), and `FLAVOR NOTES` (with flavor tags, acidity, and body meters).
  - 4-dot interactive pagination allowing dynamic switching between 4 specialty single-origin profiles (*Huila Reserve*, *Yirgacheffe Blossom*, *Antigua Volcán*, and *Tarrazú Honey*), dynamically updating cup glaze colors and tasting notes.
- **Interactive Extras**
  - **Barista Assistant Drawer**: Click the floating chat bubble on the bottom right (with notification badge `1`) to open detailed brew ratios, grind clicks, and water temperatures.
  - **Web Audio Ambiance**: Synthesizer toggle in the header for warm cafe hum, burr grind rumble, water pour, and ceramic chime.
  - **Parallax Mouse Inertia**: Interactive camera tilt that brings 3D depth to life on desktop and mobile.
  - **Sign Up Modal**: Member club onboarding.

## Running the Project

```bash
npm run dev
# or for production
npm run build
npm run start
```
Then open `http://localhost:3000` in your browser.
